## About The Project

FreeSlot is an open-source Flutter app that turns your Google Calendar into a clean picture of your free time. Insteaad of digging through event tiltes to work out when you are available, FreeSlot reads only your busy periods and shows the gaps between them, so you can answer "when are you free?" in a few seconds.

The apps never reads what your events are about - it asks Google only for busy/free information, so your event titles, guests and notes stay private.

### Demo 
[demo video](link)

### Key Features;

* **One-Tap Calendar Sync:** Sign in with Google and pull the next seven days for busy time from your primary calendar.
* **Two Ways to Look at It:** Switch between a **List** of busy periods and a **Calendar** timeline that highlights your available slots in green.
* **Privacy is Design:** Uses only the `calendar.freebusy` scope - FreeSlot can see *that* you are busy, never *why*.
* **Merged & Sorted Automatically:** Overlapping or back-to-back events are merged into single blocks, so the timeline stays readable.
* **Dark, Distraction-Free UI:** A Material 3 dark theme built for a quick glance.

The project is currently under active development, with new features being introduced regularly. As an open-source tool, FreeSlot is completely free to use, and contributions from the community are highly welcome!

### Limits 

* A Google account with a Google Calendar is required; other calendar providers are not supported yet.
* Only the **primary** calendar is read, and only the **next 7 days**.
* The calendar timeline shows one day at a time, between **08:00 and 22:00**.
* The app is not published on theApp Store or Google Play - you build and run it yourself.
